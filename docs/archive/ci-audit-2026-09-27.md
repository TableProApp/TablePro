## Mutation evidence

[Quality run 36272111324](https://github.com/cozyGarage/BookiE/actions/runs/36272111324)
at `c57ac7715` finished **failed**, while its coverage job succeeded. PostgreSQL
completed 170 mutations: 155 caught, nine missed and six unviable. Core was
interrupted after logging 88 survivors and 16 timeouts; it produced no complete
measurement. Policy, SSH and Redis were skipped and mutation artifacts were not
uploaded. The logs establish interruption, not its external cause. None of those
incomplete scopes is a pass.

The nine PostgreSQL findings led to independent size-limit expectations, direct
bounded-reader checks, a missing fractional-group rejection, a valid one-digit
scale case and exact-text preservation checks. Their local rerun is recorded in
[value contracts](../value-contracts.md). Core's broader findings remain an explicit
triage backlog; they have not been classified wholesale as equivalent or waived.

Mutation work now uses separate jobs, four core shards and two policy shards,
with fail-fast disabled. Each measurement has a shorter timeout than its job,
followed by always-run summary/upload steps. New pushes do not cancel an active
quality run. Missing/empty/unviable-only reports and failed measurements fail
visibly. Sharding and caching change execution cost, not the tested scope.

## Status and revision safeguards

Build Linux now has a final regression gate. Required jobs must succeed;
failure, cancellation, missing results and unexpected skips fail the gate.
Current-stable Clippy may be skipped only on push/pull-request events. Branch
protection is a separate repository setting and was not changed here.

Build jobs use the commit resolved by preflight. Quality jobs resolve their ref
once before launching the matrix and coverage; Flatpak uses the event SHA.
Later jobs cannot silently move to a newer branch tip. Tests cover the job-state
rules, immutable-checkout wiring, missing SSH gate and mutation report failures.

This audit does not certify every historical green run, full mutation coverage,
all live environments, package upgrade/rollback or installed Wayland behavior.
